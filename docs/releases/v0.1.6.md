Seventh test release of **Harness Designer**, with the changes asked for while building the first real harness (TC2000 engine). It's the first one that arrives as an automatic update.

## Updating from 0.1.5

- **The program offers it by itself:** when 0.1.5 opens, an **"Update to 0.1.6"** button appears in the status bar (or click the version to check). It saves pending library changes, warns about unsaved project changes, installs and opens again. Nothing gets uninstalled and your data stays.
- **Files change format.** A project or library opened with 0.1.6 is converted when it's opened, and a copy of the original is kept next to it (`<file>.v2.bak`). **After that, 0.1.5 can't open it**, so update every computer where you use the same files.
- **Connectors with no separate contact** (see below): in a library from 0.1.5, "Add standard parts" doesn't change connectors you already have. Mark the thermocouple socket and the ring terminal by hand: Library → Connectors → **Contacts: None**.

## Harness

- **Straight boot on a breakout**: Inspector of the node → Termination **"Straight boot"**, the boot, and **"On segment"**, the segment it goes on (the wide, stiff side stays at the breakout, on the side with more wires). The program checks that its cable side fits that segment, takes its leg off that segment's heat-shrink and adds it to the BOM. Costs lists it under "Breakout boots"; Checks warns if the segment isn't chosen.
- **Segments that leave a breakout backward** (toward the main connector, next to the incoming segment): Inspector of the segment → **"Leaves its breakout: Backward"**. The breakout's assembly sheet draws them on the left, above and below the incoming segment, and marks them with "←" in its list. In an arranged diagram, the branch flips to the other side of the breakout without changing lengths.
- **"↓ next"** on each wire of the pinout: copies it to the next free pin of the same connector, with the function's number + 1 ("INY Cil 1" → "INY Cil 2"), category, wire type and colour, and no destination.
- **Loose wires**: a wire can end loose at a node (type "loose" in "Goes to", or choose "loose at a node" in the Inspector). It has a route and a length, goes in the cut list and isn't an error in Checks.
- **"Goes to" by name**: the list offers "X1 · ECU:7" and "G1 · GND"; "X1:7" and "G1" still work typed by hand. Nodes show their connectors ("node 1 · Connector end · X1 Motor").
- **Splices, grounds and components**: the name can be edited in the table too.
- **Two-colour wires** ("orange/white") are drawn with the stripe in Schematic and split in the colour swatches.

## Library

- **Connectors with no separate contact**: Library → Connectors → **Contacts: "Crimped contacts" or "None"**. With "None" the wire goes straight to the part (the thermocouple's screw terminals, a ring terminal crimped on the wire): it gets no contact or seal plug, and Checks doesn't warn about them.
- **New standard parts**: MS3470A24-61S receptacle, MS3476A20-41P (20-41 insert), Delphi GT 150 3-way, Marelli SRG 140/141 loom connectors A and K, the Raychem boots 202A142, 202A163 and 222A163, and a 24 AWG Ethernet twisted pair. The engine connector's contact is M39029/4-110. Their faces are drawn by the program.
- **DT 3-way**: B and C were swapped on the face; fixed.
- **Bigger connector faces**: a button next to "Mating face / Wire side" opens the drawing over the window (Esc or a click outside closes it).
- The "Back (mm)" fields no longer stick out of the connector card, and the list no longer covers the card on narrow windows.

## Diagram and Schematic

- **Pinch zoom on the touchpad** works, and faster. Ctrl + / Ctrl − now scale the window.
- Zooming out no longer leaves traces of the blocks' borders.

## Install

On a computer without the program, download `Harness Designer_0.1.6_x64-setup.exe` below and run it.
- It installs per user and doesn't need administrator rights.
- **Windows SmartScreen may warn** because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.
