Third test release of **Harness Designer**, after building the first real harness with it. It includes everything from 0.1.1, which wasn't published.

## New

- **Assembly drawing like the workshop's hand-drawn sheet**:
  - an **overview** with the harness skeleton: main connector and numbered breakouts, with the length and heat-shrink of each run and the distance from the main connector;
  - under the overview, **one list per breakout**: name, ID, length, connector and heat-shrink of each outlet;
  - then **one sheet per breakout**, with its outlets fanned out in table-sized type.

  The whole harness on one sheet is still an option.
- **Duplicate a connector with its pinout** (Connectors → Duplicate). Identical coils or injectors are loaded once.
  - Wires to splices and grounds are kept.
  - Wires to another connector's pins are left without a destination, ready to fill in.
- **Splices have a name** (for example "SP1 · 12V+ COILS"), and in "Goes to" a splice or a ground can be found by typing part of its name.
- **"Ends at" shows only the other side of a splice**:
  - from a coil, the engine-connector pins, not the other coils;
  - from the engine connector, every coil.

  A pin that goes to a ground ends at the ground.
- **Checks grouped by type** ("Pending connectors 29"), with errors first. The Inspector shows the errors and warnings of whatever is selected.
- **Segment diameter in the Inspector**: bundle and final diameter, and the suggested heat-shrink.
- **Cut-length rounding** (Print → Cut list), always upwards so no wire comes out short.
- **Every tab remembers where it was**: scroll, selected connector, diagram zoom.
- **Pinout sheets**:
  - pending connectors show their drawing too;
  - a connector longer than a page (the 61-way engine connector) repeats its name, part and face on each page.
- **The diagram shows the connector's name first** and its ID underneath. An empty diagram has a "+ Node" button.
- **Ground points**: a ground node offers to create its ground point, with the ring lug to use.
- **Backup on save**: the previous version of the project stays next to it as `<file>.bak`.

## New standard parts

- **Engine connector MS3476L24-61P** (MIL-DTL-26482 Series 2, 61 ways), with its contact M39029/58-360 and sealing plug MS27488-20.
- **VAG 1J0 973 724** (ignition coil, 4 ways) and **VAG 1J0 973 733** (Bosch LSU 4.9 lambda, 6 ways).
- **Omega SMPW-K-F**: miniature type K thermocouple socket.
- **Mini Timer** inline connectors as named in the workshop sheet (**MINI TYMER 02W-F**, **ORIGINAL 03W_F**) and the ring lug **OJAL 16mm2 M6**. They keep those names until their real part numbers are loaded.

The program draws the faces of all of them. Like everything in the standard library, they are marked *unverified* until checked against the datasheet.

## Fixes

- The program no longer slows down as the harness grows: calculations are cached and they wait until you stop typing.
- The Wires tab opens much faster. Wire ends are typed with autocomplete, as in "Goes to".
- DTM 3-way: the cavity numbering was reversed.
- If the window reloads, the open project and library come back, including unsaved changes.

## Updating from 0.1.0

- **Your library and your projects are kept.** Projects saved with 0.1.0 open as they are, and they get the new fields (splice names, cut-length rounding) the next time you save them.
- **Adding the new standard parts**: open **Library → Add standard parts**. It only adds what's missing and doesn't change anything you already loaded.

## Install

Download `Harness Designer_0.1.2_x64-setup.exe` below and run it.
- It installs per user, over the previous version. It doesn't need administrator rights.
- **Windows SmartScreen may warn** because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.
