Second test release of **Harness Designer**.

## New

- **Three connectors in the standard library**, harness side (the VAG ones male, the thermocouple socket female):
  - **VAG 1J0 973 724**: 4-way ignition coil connector.
  - **VAG 1J0 973 733**: 6-way connector that mates the Bosch LSU 4.9 lambda sensor.
  - **Omega SMPW-K-F**: miniature type K thermocouple socket, with cavities `+` and `-`.
- **Their faces are drawn by the program**:
  - the VAG connectors are straight on the pin-1 side and rounded on the other;
  - the thermocouple socket is rectangular, with the `+` and `-` cavities.

## Updating from 0.1.0

Your library and your projects are kept. To add the new connectors to your library, open **Library → Add standard parts**. It only adds what's missing and doesn't change anything you already loaded.

Like everything in the standard library, the new parts are marked *unverified* until they are checked against the datasheet.
